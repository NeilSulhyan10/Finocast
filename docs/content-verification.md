# Finocast content verification

Company information displayed on the site was checked against the official website at https://finocast.com/ on 10 October 2026.

## Published and used

- Legal name: Fino Cast Private Limited.
- The company says it was established in 2020 and manufactures steel and alloy steel investment castings.
- Its published process is investment casting by the lost-wax method, with value added CNC machining.
- The official material application page lists pump, valve, automotive, machine tool, turbine, boiler, earth moving, medical implant, marine, power plant, food processing, dairy, textile, defence, aerospace, general engineering, automation and structural hardware applications.
- Published casting weight range: 0.050 kg to 120 kg per piece.
- Factory: Plot no. 14, Miraj MIDC, Miraj, Sangli, Maharashtra 416410, India.
- Office: S. No. 74/2, Shivaji Road, Miraj, Sangli, Maharashtra 416410, India. Nearby landmarks vary between official pages and are omitted.
- Email: info@finocast.com and bhupal@finocast.com (provided by the site owner for this update).
- Phone: +91 9822242006 (provided by the site owner for this update).

## Omitted or qualified

- The current validity of the ISO 9001:2015 certificate was not independently confirmed, so no certification claim is displayed.
- The official About page publishes facility area and annual capacity figures, but they are not displayed because current confirmation is unavailable.
- The quote and contact forms require a configured transactional email provider. Until configured, submissions return an error and direct the visitor to the published email address; the site never reports an unsent enquiry as successful.
